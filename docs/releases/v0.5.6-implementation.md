### Enrollment, state and status

Operator enrollment validates the existing private grant or Scout policy, applied
approval, original native task, owner/bot/vault/repository, source/image/pins/schema
and legacy exclusion. It launches nothing and changes no native/grant schema.
The enrollment deadline is monotonic, boot-bound and at most 24 hours. Existing
never-enrolled profiles retain predecessor behavior; attempted legacy work cannot
be enrolled to obtain a fresh allowance.

Private immutable generations form a digest chain with a separately committed
head. Missing, corrupt, oversized, public, symlinked, incompatible or interrupted
state refuses admission. Enrollment reserves metadata capacity for restrictions,
including a 4 KiB head allocation and 4 KiB emergency reserve within 1 MiB.
Shared totals use stable project identity across Scout, coder, reviewer and fresh
rework grants; reservations are never refunded after crashes or cancellation.

`/project status` authenticates and reads private native snapshots without opening
writable journals or reclaiming work. It keeps native status/run, control state,
proposal revision, verification, stop proof, original deadline and reserved counters
separate. The additional projection is capped at 3,500 characters; legacy listings
remain available. Missing provider telemetry is explicitly `unavailable`.

### Controls, stage admission and recovery

Owner-private `/project hold|continue|cancel` binds exact admitted request bytes,
applied proposal, native task and current digest token. Receipts survive failed
reply delivery and later generations; redelivery cannot replay transitions. Models
cannot issue controls. Status and controls never launch work.

Hold before admission prevents a claim/reservation; an already admitted stage may
drain its original allowance. Continue clears only a valid hold with available
budget, no uncertainty and no incompatible fence; an operator must still launch.
Cancel revokes enrollment and requests stop, including during inference/check
waits. It does not erase ownership, reservations, uncertainty or completed results.
Terminal cancellation reports `Terminal outcome unchanged`.

Coder/reviewer/scout admissions serialize against controls and lifecycle fences.
A hold committed since coder admission prevents automatic reviewer handoff even
if continue arrives before coder completion. The complete never-claimed first
review intent is retained; explicit continue and operator `--review` are required.
No reviewer allowance is charged until admission. An already admitted reviewer
retains its original deadline. Rework still requires a fresh schema-3 owner grant,
verified rejected-parent lineage, fresh checks and distinct review on the same card.
At most two coders/two reviewers and one Scout share the project allowance.

Repeated launch inspects existing facts. Crashes, lost acknowledgements, missing
resources or expired leases never prove stop and never authorize replay. Cleanup
pins exact process/resource identity; metadata-only reconciliation finalizes only
already verified evidence after independently proven stop. Native `done` alone
cannot establish verified local completion. Controls do not repair uncertain effects.

### Lifecycle compatibility

Sleep/down persists a profile-wide admission fence before inspecting work. Active,
uncertain or uninspectable work refuses shutdown before any service stops, even
without a running conversation container. The fence survives refusal/interruption.
Wake launches no project work and clears only the sleep fence after actual-release
compatibility and proven idle state. Holds, revocations, deadlines, reservations
and uncertainty persist. Old executors cannot synchronize or roll back unsupported
supervised state; preserve newest board/sidecars/grants/receipts/artifacts together.
Docker-only, cloud-primary-only routing, one poller, manual reboot wake and existing
confinement remain unchanged. No new dependency, scheduler or provider was added.
